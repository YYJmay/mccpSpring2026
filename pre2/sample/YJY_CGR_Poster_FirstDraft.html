1. Motivation & Gap

Observation 1: Most CGL methods are tailored for classification, while regression is harder and under-explored.

Observation 2: Existing assumptions often treat confounders as pure noise.

Issue: In real applications, confounders can still carry predictive signal, so forcing zero utility harms disentanglement.

Goal: Improve OOD generalization for graph-level regression by revisiting confounding effects.

OOD Robustness Causal Representation Regression

2. Framework Overview

Input Graph G
→
Learnable Masks
Causal C / Confounding S
→
Dual Readouts + Joint Loss

Enhanced GIB

L_GIB = -I(C;Y) + αI(C;G) - βI(S;Y)

Unlike prior work, the term I(S;Y) explicitly models the predictive role of confounders instead of ignoring it.

Contrastive Causal Intervention

Construct mixed representation Hmix,ij = Hc,i + Hs,j, then align (Hg,i, Hmix,ij) as positive and other Hg,k as negatives.

L = L_GIB + λL_CI

3. Why It Works

  • Balances causal compression and prediction utility.
  • Avoids collapsing confounders to “all noise”.
  • Removes label-dependence in intervention for continuous targets.
  • Improves invariance while preserving useful signal.

6. Ablation Insights

  • Ignoring confounder predictive power weakens disentanglement and OOD performance.
  • Contrastive intervention outperforms supervised intervention in robustness.
  • Both L_GIB and L_CI are complementary; using both gives the best overall performance.

7. Contributions

  • First explicit treatment of confounder predictive role in causal graph regression.
  • Generalizes class-dependent causal intervention to regression via contrastive learning.
  • Delivers consistent gains on GOOD and ReactionOOD under multiple shifts.

8. Practical Takeaways

  • When targets are continuous, avoid classification-only intervention designs.
  • Model confounders explicitly instead of forcing them to be non-predictive.
  • Use representation-level intervention for label-scarce or label-inapplicable settings.

9. Limitations & Future Work

  • Hyperparameter sensitivity exists for λ; careful tuning remains important.
  • Smaller datasets may still challenge causal disentanglement quality.
  • Future: combine with stronger pretraining and evaluate on broader graph domains.
