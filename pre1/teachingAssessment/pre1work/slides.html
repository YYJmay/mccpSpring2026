CONTENT & STRUCTURE KEY FINDINGS
Why Regression Breaks Prior Methods

Classification

  • "Is this toxic? YES or NO"
  • Finite categories → group by label
  • Prior methods mix graphs within same class
vs

Regression

  • "How toxic? 37.2 on a scale"
  • Continuous values → no natural groups
  • Label-based intervention breaks
Second problem: Prior methods treat confounders as pure noise — but molecular weight is predictive, just not causal. Ignoring it hurts separation.
We solved both problems. Let me show you how.

KEY FINDINGS EVIDENCE
Insight 1: Don't Discard Confounders — Understand Them
  • Standard: force causal part to capture ALL signal, treat confounder as noise
  • Ours: let confounders predict too — causal part captures only causal signal
  • Result: much cleaner separation of cause vs correlation
Enhanced GIB = "Let confounders predict too → cleaner causal/confounding separation"

Insight 2: Contrastive Learning Replaces Labels
  • Mix causal part of graph A with random confounder of B → "counterfactual"
  • Positive pair: original A and its counterfactual (same cause)
  • Negative pairs: all other graphs in the batch
